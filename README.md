# SkyShowtime Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/skyshowtime-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more SkyShowtime users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of SkyShowtime's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Keep the ad’s promise on the first screen | +10–20% CR to subscription |
| 02 | Show the show before the price | +5–15% CR to subscription |
| 03 | Make the yearly saving impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU, from more yearly plans |
| 04 | Your plans in your words, proof where they decide | +5–15% CR to subscription |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +5–10% CR to subscription |

Every price, rating and feature comes from SkyShowtime's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for SkyShowtime. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
